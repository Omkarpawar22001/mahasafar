About Temple

Visiting Bhimashankar Temple feels like stepping away from the busy world and entering a peaceful natural retreat. Located deep within the Sahyadri hills, the journey itself is as special as the destination. Surrounded by dense forests, mist, and fresh mountain air, the temple offers a perfect mix of spirituality and nature. It is one of those places where you don't just go for darshan, but also to relax your mind and reconnect with yourself.

History & Significance

Bhimashankar is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, making it highly important in Hindu belief. According to legend, Lord Shiva took the form of Bhimashankar to defeat the demon Tripurasura, and the energy of that battle is believed to remain here. The temple is ancient and has been a place of worship for centuries, attracting devotees from across India. Its connection to both mythology and spirituality makes it a deeply respected pilgrimage site.

What Makes It Unique

Discover the unique factors that set Bhimashankar Temple apart from other shrines.

Sanctuary Setting

Located deep inside a dense forest reserve sanctuary, protecting rare flora and fauna.

Nagara Stone Architecture

Features a classic Nagara style design constructed with black basalt stone and detailed carvings.

Forest Trekking Routes

Highly popular among trekkers, offering scenic trails like Shidi Ghat and Ganesh Ghat.

Holy Jyotirlinga

One of the 12 sacred pillars of light of Lord Shiva, carrying powerful spiritual vibrations.

Travel Guide

Convenient ways to reach Bhimashankar Temple in the Sahyadri Range.

Option 1: From Pune (By Road)

Bhimashankar is about 110 km from Pune via Manchar. It takes around 3–4 hours by car or bike, making it a highly popular weekend getaway.

Pune: ~3-4 hours

Option 2: From Mumbai (By Road)

Bhimashankar is around 220 km from Mumbai via the Expressway and Manchar, taking approximately 5–6 hours by car.

Mumbai: ~5-6 hours

Option 3: Trekking Routes

For adventure lovers, trekking routes start from Khandas village near Karjat. The climb goes through lush green hills and waterfalls during the monsoon.

Essential Information

Important details regarding timings, fees, and the ideal season to visit.

Timings & Crowds

Opens daily at 4:30 AM and closes at 9:30 PM. Early morning visits are perfect for peaceful darshan and avoiding heavy crowds.

Entry & Rituals

General entry is completely free. Dedicated counters are available for performing special rituals like abhishek or archana.

Best Time to Visit

October to February offers the most comfortable weather. The monsoons are exceptionally scenic with green valleys and mist.
