About Temple

Visiting Ekvira Devi Temple is a mix of devotion and a small physical journey that makes the experience more meaningful. Located on a hill near Lonavala, you have to climb around 200 steps to reach the temple, and with every step, the surroundings start becoming quieter and more scenic. The temple is especially important for the Koli community, and you can feel a strong sense of faith and tradition here. It's a perfect place if you want a short spiritual trip combined with a bit of nature.

History & Significance

Ekvira Devi is believed to be a form of Goddess Renuka and is worshipped as a powerful and protective deity. The temple has been a place of devotion for centuries and is closely connected to local traditions and communities. It is also located near the ancient Karla Caves, which adds historical importance to the entire area. Many devotees visit regularly to seek blessings for strength, protection, and success.

What Makes It Unique

Discover the unique factors that set Ekvira Devi Temple apart from other shrines.

Hilltop Location

Perched on a high hill near Lonavala, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding valleys and green slopes.

Karla Caves Connection

Shares the hill with the famous 2nd-century BC Buddhist Karla Caves, combining history and spirituality.

The 200-Step Climb

An energetic walk up 200 stone steps surrounded by scenic views and local vendors selling fresh offerings.

Koli Culture & Faith

Serves as the main family deity (Kuldaivat) for the fisherman community, reflecting vibrant local customs.

Travel Guide

Convenient ways to reach Ekvira Devi Temple from Mumbai and Pune.

Option 1: By Road (Car/Cab)

Easily accessible from both Mumbai and Pune via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. Drive to the base of the hill at Karla village. From Mumbai, it takes around 2 hours, and from Pune about 1–1.5 hours.

Mumbai: ~2 hrs | Pune: ~1.5 hrs

Option 2: By Train

Take a local train to Lonavala Railway Station (if travelling from Pune) or an express train from Mumbai. From Lonavala station, hire an auto-rickshaw or local taxi directly to the base village of Karla.

Lonavala Station: 11 km away

Option 3: The Ascent

Park at the base village of Karla and begin the climb. The trail features approximately 200 stone steps that are well-maintained and flanked by shops.

Essential Information

Important details regarding timings, fees, and the ideal season to visit.

Timings & Crowds

Opens early morning around 5:00 AM and closes around 8:00 PM. Mornings are the best time to visit to avoid crowds and hot weather.

Entry & Charges

Entry to the temple is completely free. Small parking charges apply at the base of the hill, and local pooja offerings are optional.

Best Time to Visit

Monsoon (June to September) and winter (October to March) are ideal. Monsoon turns the entire landscape into a lush green paradise.
