About Temple

Visiting Parli Vaijnath Temple feels calm, traditional, and deeply spiritual, especially if you are exploring Jyotirlinga temples. Unlike very crowded pilgrimage places, this temple gives you a chance to experience darshan in a more peaceful and less rushed way. The environment around the temple is simple and quiet, which makes it easier to focus on prayer and spend some time in reflection.

History & Significance

Parli Vaijnath Temple is believed by many devotees to be one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, though there are different beliefs regarding its exact position among the twelve. The temple has a long history and has been an important place of worship for centuries. It is associated with various legends related to Lord Shiva and healing, which is why the name "Vaijnath" is often connected with the idea of a divine healer.

What Makes It Unique

Discover the unique factors that set Parli Vaijnath Temple apart from other shrines.

Traditional Structure

Beautiful, rustic stone construction reflecting classical Marathwada design.

Hilltop Approach

Built on a small hill, offering a gradual transition into a peaceful and sacred area.

Close Darshan

Allows devotees to approach closely to the Shiva linga for a highly personal experience.

Serene Setting

Less commercialized compared to other shrines, offering a quiet environment for prayer.

Travel Guide

Convenient ways to reach the temple in Parli.

Option 1: Railways & local Transport

Parli town has its own dedicated railway station (Parli Vaijnath Station) connected to major hubs. Cabs and shared autos are readily available outside to reach the temple.

Rail Hub: Parli Railway Station

Option 2: Driving Routes

From Mumbai, it is around 500-550 km (~8-10 hours drive). From Pune, it is around 400-450 km (~7-8 hours drive) via the Latur-Beed state highways.

Pune (7-8 hrs) | Mumbai (8-10 hrs)

Option 3: Public State Buses

MSRTC operates regular state transport buses from Beed, Latur, Nanded, and Aurangabad directly to Parli bus stand.

Essential Information

Important details regarding timings, queues, and ideal visiting slots.

Timings & Crowds

Opens at 5:00 AM and closes at 9:00 PM. Morning hours are highly recommended when crowds are minimal and the weather is cool.

Entry & Rituals

General entry for darshan is completely free. Special abhishek and pooja services managed by the trust are available at the counters.

Best Time to Visit

October to March offers comfortable weather. Summers can be quite hot in Marathwada, while monsoons bring light showers.
