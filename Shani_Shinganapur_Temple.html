About Temple

Visiting Shani Shingnapur Temple feels completely different from a normal temple visit because the entire place is built around belief rather than structure. As you enter the village, you’ll notice something unusual – many houses traditionally don’t have doors or locks. This itself gives you an idea of how deeply people trust Lord Shani’s protection. The temple is open and simple, without heavy decoration, yet the atmosphere feels strong and powerful. It’s a place where you don’t go for beauty, but for faith and belief in justice.

History & Significance

The temple is dedicated to Lord Shani, who is known as the god of karma and justice. According to local belief, the black stone idol of Lord Shani was discovered naturally and was not installed in the usual temple way. It is believed that the deity chose this place himself, which is why there is no roof over the idol. Over time, the temple became famous across India for its strong spiritual belief and the idea that Lord Shani protects the village from theft and wrongdoing.

What Makes It Unique

Discover the unique factors that set Shani Shingnapur Temple apart from other shrines.

No Doors or Locks

Legendary village tradition where homes remain doorless, trusting the protection of Shani.

Roofless Platform

The black stone idol rests entirely in the open air, symbolizing truth and transparency.

Oil Offerings

Devotees perform oil abhishek (pouring oil) directly onto the naturally discovered stone idol.

Rural Setting

Located in Shingnapur village, surrounded by quiet fields and simple farming settlements.

Travel Guide

Convenient ways to reach the temple from nearby locations.

Option 1: Combo Trip from Shirdi

Located just 35 km from Shirdi and 70 km from Ahmednagar. It is highly convenient to combine this visit with a Shirdi trip by hiring local cabs or taking frequent tour buses.

Distance: 35 km from Shirdi

Option 2: Direct Driving Routes

From Mumbai, it takes around 5–6 hours by car, and from Pune around 3–4 hours via the well-connected state highways passing through Ahmednagar.

Pune (3-4 hrs) | Mumbai (5-6 hrs)

Option 3: Public Transport Buses

State transport (MSRTC) buses and shared jeeps operate regularly between Ahmednagar, Rahuri, Shirdi, and Shingnapur village.

Essential Information

Important details regarding timings, queues, and ideal visiting slots.

Timings & Saturdays

Open 24/7 as an open-air shrine. Saturdays are highly auspicious and attract very large crowds; weekdays are ideal for a peaceful visit.

Entry & Offerings

General entry for darshan is completely free. Devotees regularly purchase oil to pour onto the deity, which is optional.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is ideal. Summers can be quite hot, especially since the entire temple is open and directly exposed to sunlight.

Location & Coordinates
