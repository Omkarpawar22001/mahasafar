OUR STORY

Driven by a passion for discovery, safety, and community in the mountains.

Redefining Outdoor Exploration

Welcome to Maha Safar — a leading premium outdoor adventure and heritage guide organization in Maharashtra. Founded by a dedicated cohort of mountaineers and local historians, we are committed to changing how explorers experience Maharashtra's raw geography, culture, and ancient history.

We curate premium escapes designed for a wide range of travelers. Whether you want to conquer rugged Sahyadri ridge routes, explore pristine seasonal waterfalls, camp under starry skies, or uncover the history of ancient forts and temples, we ensure your journey is safe, organized, and unforgettable.

How We Began

A decade ago, the true trails of the Sahyadri range were known to only a few. Verified trail data was scarce, local guiding was disorganized, and safety standards were inconsistent. This left many eager outdoor lovers hesitant to explore the wild.

Our journey began in the mountains. Armed with a notebook and a GPS tracker, our founders set out to map and document over 50 offbeat routes, mountain fort corridors, and hidden waterfall tracks. We wanted to make the wilderness simpler, safer, and accessible to anyone seeking adventure.

Today, we strive to build a community where college students on a budget and busy professionals looking to escape city life can step outdoors with confidence. By combining premium wilderness first-aid-certified guides, high-quality gear, and delicious, authentic local meals, we offer budget-friendly travel experiences that prioritize your peace of mind.

Our Core Principles

We believe that outdoor adventure is a powerful tool to reconnect with nature, learn history, and push personal boundaries. We guide every trip with three core principles:

Safety & Preparation

We never cut corners. Our guides are trained first-responders, our paths are thoroughly vetted, and we carry proper communication and safety equipment on every single trek.

Eco-Tourism & Stewardship

We are guests in nature. We strictly follow "Leave No Trace" principles, clean up trails as we go, and teach travelers how to respect and preserve biodiversity.

Community & Local Support

We grow together. We partner directly with villagers at our base camps, employing them as local guides and sourcing authentic home-cooked meals to support rural economies.
