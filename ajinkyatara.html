About Fort

Ajinkyatara Fort is a historic hill fort located in Satara city, Maharashtra. Known as the 'Star Fort', it offers a perfect blend of history, easy accessibility, and panoramic views of Satara city.

The fort can be reached by road, making it ideal for beginners, families, and visitors seeking a quick historical getaway. First built in the 12th century by the Shilahara dynasty, it has stood as a silent witness to major historical power shifts in Maharashtra.

Major Attractions

Explore the historical structures and city views of Ajinkyatara Fort.

Fort Walls & Gates

Historic stone ramparts and gates that have protected the 'Star Fort' for centuries.

Satara City View

Incredible panoramic bird's-eye views of the entire Satara city layout below.

Temples on Top

Dedicated stone temples located on the tableland, including the Ajinkyadevi Temple.

Sunset Viewpoint

A popular viewpoint offering stunning sunset vistas over the Sahyadri mountains.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Transit from Satara

Take a train or bus from Mumbai to Satara city. From Satara station or city stand, local auto-rickshaws and taxis are easily available to go directly up the fort.

Cost: ₹100 – ₹250 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

A scenic ~260 km road trip from Mumbai via the Pune-Satara Highway (NH 48). A fully motorable road is available almost all the way to the top, with secure vehicle parking spaces near the fort.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Overnight stay is not allowed on the fort. Abundant comfortable hotels and dining options are available in Satara city, which sits right at the foot of the fort.

Essentials

Wear comfortable walking shoes. Carry water bottles, a cap, sunscreen, and a camera to capture the breathtaking sunset and Satara city vistas.

Best Time

October to February is ideal for pleasant cooler weather. Monsoon (June-Sept) is also very popular, when the fort is covered in lush green grass and mist.

Location & Coordinates
