About Trek

Andharban Trek, meaning 'Dark Forest', is one of the most scenic jungle treks in Maharashtra located near Pimpri village in Tamhini Ghat. It is famous for its dense forest cover, misty trails, waterfalls, and unique descending route.

The trek offers stunning views of Kundalika Valley and the surrounding Sahyadri ranges, making it ideal for nature lovers and photographers. Since the entire trail is covered by a thick forest canopy, very little sunlight reaches the ground, giving the jungle its mysterious name.

Trek Type

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the dense Tamhini jungle route.

Dense Forest Trail

An amazing trek completely covered by a thick green canopy of trees, keeping it cool and dark even in afternoon hours.

Kundalika Valley Views

Splendid lookouts over the deep gorges and green ridges of the Kundalika river valley.

Waterfalls & Streams

Numerous streams, rivers, and seasonal waterfalls that you cross along the trail.

Misty Jungle Paths

Stunning misty trail experiences during peak monsoon, turning the dark forest into a fairy tale.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the starting base village from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Cab via Pimpri

Take a Mumbai local train to Lonavala station. From Lonavala, hire a private cab/taxi directly to Pimpri village (~50 km), where the trek begins near the Pimpri Dam area.

Cost: ₹400 – ₹800 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive to Pimpri Base

A scenic ~130 km road trip via Lonavala and the Mulshi-Tamhini Ghat road. Note that since it's a point-to-point descending trek starting at Pimpri village and ending at Bhira village, you must arrange return transit or hire a driver to bring your vehicle to Bhira.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

No accommodations or food options are available inside the forest trail. Carry your own packed lunch. Return transport must be pre-arranged at the end point in Bhira village.

Essentials

Carry 3 liters water, trekking shoes with excellent mud grip, a high-quality raincoat, extra dry clothes, insect/mosquito repellent, a torch/headlamp, and a first aid kit.

Best Time

June to September (Monsoon) is the absolute best time to experience the roaring waterfalls, lush greenery, and heavy mist. Forest permits are required to enter the sanctuary.

Location & Coordinates

Andharban Trek, Pimpri Village, Tamhini Ghat, Maharashtra, India
