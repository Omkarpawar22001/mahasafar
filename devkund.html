About Devkund Waterfall

Devkund Waterfall is one of Maharashtra's most beautiful plunge waterfalls, located near Bhira village in Raigad district. The name Devkund means 'Pond of Gods' and comes from the crystal-clear turquoise pool formed at the base of the waterfall. Standing approximately 80–100 feet tall, the waterfall is surrounded by dense forests, rocky cliffs, and seasonal streams. It is fed by the Kundalika River and is among the most popular trekking destinations in Maharashtra.

History and Significance

Considered sacred by local villagers, Devkund derives its name from its spiritual and natural significance. Located in the biodiversity-rich Western Ghats, it is famous for its crystal-clear blue-green water, scenic jungle trek, and ecological importance.

What You'll Experience

Explore the highlights of the hidden jungle trail and adventure activities at Devkund.

1. Devkund Waterfall

Standing approximately 80–100 feet tall, the waterfall plunges down into a crystal-clear turquoise blue pool, offering a breathtaking and sacred sight.

2. Jungle Trek

An immersive trek through the dense and biodiversity-rich forests of the Western Ghats, crossing seasonal streams and rocky pathways.

3. Kundalika River

The beautiful river feeding Devkund Waterfall, offering rich aquatic sights and serving as a key river source in Raigad district.

4. Tamhini Ghat Views

Breathtaking mountain views of surrounding ridges, deep valleys, and beautiful green passes visible along the trek route.

5. Sunrise and Monsoon Landscapes

Beautiful landscapes highlighting mist-filled Sahyadri valleys and fresh greenery during the sunrise hours and wet monsoon seasons.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach Bhira base village from Mumbai and Pune.

By Train

The nearest transit stations are Lonavala Railway Station and Khopoli Railway Station. From there, you can hire private cabs, shared autos, or local buses to reach Bhira village.

By Bus

From Pune: Pune → Paud → Mulshi → Tamhini → Bhira

From Mumbai: Mumbai → Khopoli → Pali → Bhira

By Car

From Pune: Pune → Chandani Chowk → Mulshi → Tamhini → Bhira

From Mumbai: Mumbai → Panvel → Khopoli → Pali → Bhira

Best Time to Visit

Plan your trek according to the seasons to experience Devkund at its best.

Monsoon Season (June – September)

During the monsoons, the waterfall transforms into a massive, roaring force of nature surrounded by lush greenery and seasonal streams. Note that trails can be muddy and slippery, and treks may be closed by authorities during extreme rainfall warnings.

Post-Monsoon (October – February) — Best Overall

The ideal time to visit Devkund. The water flow is gentle and safe, and the plunge pool turns into a signature crystal-clear turquoise blue. The weather is cool and pleasant for trekking.

Summer Season (March – May)

The weather is hot, and water levels recede significantly. Trekking during early morning hours is recommended to avoid heat.

Safety Guidelines

Avoid visiting during heavy rainfall alerts or red alerts issued by local authorities.

Never stand directly below the strong waterfall plunge flow due to force and falling debris.

Wear anti-slip trekking shoes to navigate slippery rocks and stream crossings.

Stay with your group and do not wander off into deep sections of the forest reserve.

Start early to complete the trek and return before sunset hours.

Do not litter. Keep the forest ecosystem clean and eco-friendly.

Always follow local guide instructions and stay within permitted safety zones.

Nearby Attractions

Extend your adventure to these scenic spots located around Bhira village.

Tamhini Ghat

A breathtaking mountain passage famous for scenic valleys, massive green landscapes, and numerous seasonal roadside waterfalls during monsoon months.

Mulshi Lake

A large, serene reservoir formed by the Mulshi Dam, offering camping, boating, and scenic resorts on its shores.

Kundalika River & Bhira Dam

Bhira Dam (the base village site) feeds the Kundalika River, popular across India for white-water river rafting and camping tours.
