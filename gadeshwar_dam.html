About Gadeshwar Dam

Gadeshwar Dam feels like a quiet escape hidden among the hills near Panvel. It is a popular monsoon and weekend destination known for its scenic water views, greenery, and peaceful atmosphere. It is the perfect spot for travelers seeking a refreshing day out amidst nature without going too far from the city.

Exact Location

The dam is located near Gadeshwar village in Panvel, Maharashtra. Reaching Gadeshwar Dam is simple and quick. As it is close to Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Panvel, it is ideal for a short road trip during weekends or holidays.

What Makes It Unique

Explore the unique highlights and landscape features of Gadeshwar Dam.

Wide Dam Reservoir

Enjoy the peaceful sight of wide dam waters stretching out against the mountains.

Lush Green Hills

Beautiful hills frame the water body, turning extremely vibrant green in the monsoon.

Cascading Water

Watch water currents flow over rock patches and stream outlets during the heavy rains.

Quiet Sanctuary

A serene atmosphere away from the city crowd, perfect for nature photography.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive transport options to reach the Gadeshwar Dam area.

Option 1: By Car (Best Option)

Traveling by car is the best option. From Mumbai or Navi Mumbai, drive via the Mumbai–Pune Expressway or Sion–Panvel Highway towards Panvel and follow local roads to the dam.

Direct Road Connection

Option 2: By Train

The nearest railway station is Panvel or Kharghar. From there, you can hire an auto-rickshaw or taxi to reach the dam.

Nearest Station: Panvel / Kharghar

Option 3: By Bus

Buses are available to Panvel and Kharghar. From the bus stop, local transport can take you closer to the destination.

Essential Information

Details regarding physical access, budget, and the main attractions.

Access / Walk

There is no trek required. You can directly reach the dam area by road and enjoy the surroundings with a short walk.

Budget & Costs

This is a very budget-friendly trip. Travel mainly consists of fuel or local transport costs, and there is generally no entry fee.

What to See

The main attraction is the wide dam water surrounded by green hills, especially during the monsoon season.
