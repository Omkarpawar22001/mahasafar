About Plateau

Garbett Plateau is one of the most scenic trekking destinations near Matheran in Maharashtra. It is known for its vast green plateau, stunning valley views, and misty landscapes during monsoon.

The trek offers a perfect mix of forest trails, open ridges, and panoramic viewpoints, making it ideal for beginners and nature lovers. The route connects Bhivpuri to Matheran, offering options to return via Matheran point.

Region

Raigad District

Access

Bhivpuri Road

Major Attractions

Explore the scenic beauty and attractions of the Garbett Plateau.

Garbett Plateau

A vast flat expanse of green tableland offering 360-degree views of the surrounding hills.

Matheran Hills View

Spectacular sights of Matheran's rising green ranges and vertical valley drops.

Monsoon Waterfalls

Numerous seasonal streams and waterfalls cascading down the steep slopes.

Forest Trail & Ridge Walk

A thrilling walk along the narrow mountain ridges and shady forest pathways.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: The Rail Route

Board a local train from Mumbai (Central Line) to Bhivpuri Road Station (~2 hrs). The trek starts directly from outside the railway station. An alternative route is via Neral Station using local transport.

Cost: ₹100 – ₹200 Approx

Option 2: The Bus Route

Take a bus to Neral or Karjat, then hire a local shared auto-rickshaw or taxi to reach Bhivpuri Road or Matheran foothill.

Cost: ₹150 – ₹350 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~80 km drive from Mumbai (2–3 hrs) via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway route or Karjat Road. You can park near Bhivpuri Road Station or Dasturi Naka parking in Matheran.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

No food or stay is available along the trek route itself. Comfortable hotels, resorts, and lodges are abundantly available at the top in Matheran.

Essentials

Carry water, sturdy trekking shoes, a raincoat (mandatory in monsoon), a cap, sunscreen, and a basic first aid kit.

Best Time

June to February is ideal. The monsoon season (June-Sept) features lush green landscapes and waterfalls, while the winter months (Oct-Feb) offer cool weather.

Location & Coordinates

Garbett Plateau, Near Matheran, Raigad District, Maharashtra, India
