About Peak

Kalsubai Peak is the highest peak in Maharashtra at 1646 meters above sea level. Located near Bhandardara, it is one of the state's most popular trekking destinations.

Famous for its metal ladders, scenic landscapes, sunrise views, and panoramic Sahyadri vistas, Kalsubai is ideal for both fit beginners and experienced trekkers. The peak offers a grand sense of accomplishment when you reach the small temple honoring Kalsubai Devi at the summit.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of Maharashtra's highest summit trail.

Summit Temple

A small red temple dedicated to the local deity Kalsubai Devi sitting at the absolute highest point in the state.

Metal Ladders

Four iron ladder sections built over sheer vertical rock faces to make the steep climbing patches safer for trekkers.

Bhandardara Lake Views

Grand high-altitude lookouts over the expansive shimmering blue waters of Arthur Hill Lake (Bhandardara).

Sahyadri Panorama

360-degree views of surrounding forts like Alang-Madan-Kulang, Harishchandragad, and Ratangad.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the base village from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Jeep (Bari Village)

Take a Mumbai local train to Kasara station (~2 hours). From Kasara, hire a shared passenger jeep directly to Bari village, the starting point of the trek.

Cost: ₹200 – ₹450 Approx

Option 2: Transit via Igatpuri

Alternatively, take a train or bus to Igatpuri. From Igatpuri, board local state transport (ST) buses heading towards Bhandardara and get down at Bari village.

Cost: ₹250 – ₹550 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~160 km drive from Mumbai along the Mumbai-Nasik Highway, exiting at Ghoti town towards Bhandardara and Bari village. Secure vehicle parking is available at the base village.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Overnight stays are not allowed at the summit temple. Excellent rustic stays, homestays, and camp options operate in Bari village at the base. Local meals are available at the base and trail stalls.

Essentials

Carry 3–4L water, good trekking shoes, a flashlight/torch (highly critical for night/sunrise treks), a warm jacket, a raincoat, and a basic first aid kit.

Best Time

October to February is ideal. Monsoon (June-Sept) is also extremely popular for flower blooms and heavy fog, but expect high wind speeds and rain on the ladders.

Location & Coordinates

Kalsubai Peak, Near Bhandardara, Ahmednagar District, Maharashtra, India
