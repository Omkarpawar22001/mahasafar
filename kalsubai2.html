About Temple

Tulja Bhavani Temple is one of the most respected and visited temples in Maharashtra, located in the holy town of Tuljapur in Dharashiv district. Dedicated to Goddess Tulja Bhavani, the temple attracts thousands of devotees every day and lakhs during major festivals. For many Maharashtrian families, the Goddess is their Kuldevi, making this temple an important part of their family traditions.

The temple stands on the Balaghat hills and has been a center of faith for centuries. The surrounding streets are lined with shops selling flowers, coconuts, and religious items. The temple's ancient stone architecture adds to its spiritual charm. A visit to Tulja Bhavani Temple offers a beautiful blend of spirituality, culture, and history.

Significance

Kuldevi of Maharashtra

Location

Balaghat Hills

History & Significance

Tulja Bhavani Temple has a rich history that dates back several centuries and is deeply rooted in Hindu traditions. According to local legends, Goddess Bhavani appeared to protect her devotee Anubhuti from a powerful demon. After defeating the demon, the Goddess chose Tuljapur as her sacred abode.

The temple gained immense importance during the Maratha period and is closely associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. It is believed that Shivaji Maharaj was a devoted follower of Tulja Bhavani and sought her blessings before important battles. Because of this connection, the temple holds a special place in Maharashtra's history.

Royal Link

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Symbol

Bhavani Sword

Main Deity

The main deity worshipped here is Goddess Tulja Bhavani, who is considered a powerful form of Adi Shakti. The idol is carved from black stone and is believed to be self-manifested according to local beliefs. The Goddess is depicted with multiple arms holding divine weapons that symbolize her power to destroy evil and protect her devotees.

Her fierce yet compassionate form attracts devotees from different parts of the country. The idol is beautifully decorated with flowers, sarees, ornaments, and traditional jewelry throughout the day. During special occasions, the deity is adorned with unique decorations that draw large crowds. The divine presence inside the sanctum creates a peaceful and spiritual experience for visitors.

Temple Timings

The temple generally opens in the early morning and remains accessible throughout the day for devotees.

Morning Darshan

Opening: Around 5:00 AM

Morning Slot: 5:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Afternoon Darshan

Afternoon Slot: 12:30 PM – 6:30 PM

Temple may close briefly for rituals between sessions.

Evening Darshan

Evening Slot: 7:00 PM onwards

Closing: Around 9:30 PM

Timings may change during Navratri and special religious occasions. Early morning visits are recommended for a peaceful darshan and shorter waiting times.

Aarti & Puja Schedule

The temple follows a daily schedule of rituals and prayers that attract devotees from across the region.

Morning Rituals

Kakad Aarti: Early Morning

Abhishek Puja: Morning Hours

Daytime Rituals

Alankar Puja: Before Noon

Mahanaivedya: Afternoon Offering

Evening Rituals

Evening Aarti: After Sunset

Shej Aarti: Before Temple Closing

Many devotees prefer attending the Kakad Aarti, as the early morning atmosphere inside the temple is considered especially sacred and peaceful.

How to Reach

Tuljapur is well connected by road and can be easily reached from major cities in Maharashtra.

By Road

Tuljapur is well connected via national and state highways. Mumbai is approximately 450 km, Pune is approximately 300 km, and Solapur is approximately 45 km away.

Solapur to Tuljapur: ~45 km

By Train

The nearest major railway station is Solapur, from where taxis and buses are easily available to Tuljapur. Solapur station is well connected to Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and other major cities.

Nearest Junction: Solapur Station

By Bus

Regular MSRTC buses operate from Mumbai, Pune, Solapur, Latur, Dharashiv, and nearby cities directly to Tuljapur bus stand.

By Air

The nearest major airport is Pune Airport, while Solapur Airport is the closest airport to Tuljapur. Taxis are available from both airports.

Nearest Airport: Solapur / Pune

Essential Visitor Info

Important details to ensure a smooth and comfortable temple visit.

Dress Code

Visitors should wear decent and comfortable clothing as the temple is an important religious site. Footwear must be removed before entering the temple premises.

Photography

Photography is generally not permitted inside the main sanctum area. Keep your phone on silent mode to maintain the peaceful atmosphere.

Crowd Tips

During weekends and festivals, waiting times may be longer. Senior citizens and families with children should plan visits during less crowded hours. Keep personal belongings secure.

Facilities Available

The temple offers several facilities to make the visit comfortable for devotees and tourists.

Parking & Access

Large parking areas are available for private vehicles and buses. Shoe-keeping facilities are available near the entrance for convenience.

Stay & Food

Several hotels, lodges, and guest houses are located close to the temple. Numerous prasad counters offer traditional offerings and religious items. The local market provides easy access to food and snacks.

Services

Clean washrooms, drinking water facilities, and basic hygiene facilities are available. Medical assistance is often available during large gatherings and festivals. Security arrangements are maintained for visitor safety.

Festivals & Special Events

Major celebrations that transform Tuljapur into a vibrant center of devotion.

Navratri

The most important festival, attracting lakhs of devotees. The temple is beautifully decorated with flowers, lights, and traditional decorations.

Dussehra

Another major occasion when large numbers of devotees visit the temple to celebrate the victory of good over evil.

Chaitra Navratri

Holds great significance among worshippers. Special aartis, ceremonies, and cultural programs are organized throughout.

Daily Rituals

Special arrangements are made by authorities to manage the crowd. The spiritual energy creates a unique atmosphere.

Nearby Attractions

Combine your temple visit with these interesting attractions located nearby.

Naldurg Fort

Known for its massive walls and impressive historical architecture, Naldurg Fort is one of the most popular attractions near Tuljapur.

Akkalkot Swami Temple

An important spiritual destination associated with Swami Samarth Maharaj. A must-visit for devotees in the region.

Dharashiv Caves & Siddheshwar

Explore ancient rock-cut Dharashiv Caves and the scenic Siddheshwar Temple beside a beautiful lake in Solapur.
