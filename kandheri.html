About Fort

Kandheri Fort is a historic island fort located in the Arabian Sea near Alibaug. Developed by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj to strengthen naval defense, the fort offers a unique combination of sea travel, history, and exploration.

It features a lighthouse, ancient cannons, fort walls, and stunning views of the Arabian Sea. Originally fortified in 1679, the island fort played a vital role in the Maratha navy under Kanhoji Angre, countering foreign marine forces.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the marine defense island.

Kandheri Lighthouse

A towering British-built lighthouse erected in 1867, standing tall in the center of the island.

Cannons & Ramparts

Well-preserved heavy iron cannons resting on the strong perimeter basalt sea walls of the fort.

Arabian Sea Views

Panoramic lookouts over the infinite horizon and rolling blue waves of the ocean.

Underi Fort View

Beautiful sightlines overlooking the neighboring sister island fort of Underi, built on rocky reefs.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the island fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Ferry & Local Boat (Via Thal Village)

Take a passenger ferry from Gateway of India to Mandwa Jetty. From Mandwa, travel to Thal village by local bus/auto. From Thal beach, hire local fishermen boats to take you across to Kandheri Fort (~20–30 minutes boat ride).

Cost: ₹400 – ₹800 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive to Thal / Alibaug

A scenic ~100–120 km drive from Mumbai to Alibaug and onwards to Thal beach village. Leave your vehicle at the Thal beach parking area and proceed to take a local boat. Boat availability strictly depends on weather and tide conditions.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth sea adventure.

Stay & Food

No overnight stays or food stalls exist on the island. Excellent hotels, seaside resorts, and Konkani dining houses are available in Alibaug city, 8 km away.

Essentials

Carry sufficient drinking water, dry snacks, a cap/sun hat, sunglasses, comfortable flat footwear for walking on wet rocks, a camera, and extra clothes.

Best Time

October to March is ideal for calm sea currents. Boats do not operate during monsoon season (June to September) as the sea gets extremely rough and dangerous.
