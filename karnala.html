About Fort

Karnala Fort is a scenic hill fort located inside the Karnala Bird Sanctuary near Panvel, Maharashtra. It is famous for its unique thumb-shaped rock pinnacle, lush greenery, and rich biodiversity.

The fort is a popular one-day trekking destination near Mumbai and is ideal for beginners, bird watchers, photographers, and nature lovers.

History

12th Century

Access

Sanctuary Trek

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the bird sanctuary and historical fort ruins.

Karnala Pinnacle

The iconic thumb-shaped basalt rock pinnacle rising 38 meters from the center of the fort.

Bird Sanctuary Trails

Lush green forest pathways rich in biodiversity and ideal for birdwatching.

Fort Ruins & Structures

Dating back to the Yadavas, the gates, chambers, and reservoirs tell tales of the past.

Wildlife & Greenery

Home to over 150 resident and migratory bird species, langurs, and beautiful wild flora.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Transit

Take a local harbor line train from Mumbai (CSMT/Dadar) to Panvel Station (~1–1.5 hrs). From Panvel, frequent state transport (ST) buses and shared auto-rickshaws connect directly to the Karnala Bird Sanctuary entrance (~12 km).

Cost: ₹100 – ₹200 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

A scenic ~50 km drive from Mumbai along the Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH 66). The road takes you directly to the entrance of the Karnala Sanctuary where paid parking is available.

Cost: ₹500 – ₹1000 Approx

Option 3: The Sanctuary Trek

The trek begins inside the bird sanctuary gate. It takes about 1.5–2 hours of steady walking along a well-defined forest trail (approx. 3 km) to reach the fort ruins.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Comfortable hotels and homestays are available in Panvel city. Food is limited to basic local snacks near the sanctuary gate; it is highly recommended to carry your own food and water up the fort.

Essentials

Carry 2–3L water, strong trekking shoes, energy bars, and a raincoat (in monsoon). Binoculars are a must-have for bird watching. Ensure to carry mosquito repellent.

Best Time

June to February is ideal. The monsoon (June-Sept) brings lush greenery and waterfalls, while the winter months (Oct-Feb) are the peak season for spotting migratory birds.

Location & Coordinates

Karnala Fort, Panvel, Raigad, Maharashtra
