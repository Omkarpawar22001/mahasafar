About Malhargad Fort

Malhargad Fort, also known as Sonori Fort, is a historic hill fort situated near the village of Sonori, about 30 km from Pune. It holds a distinct place in history as the very last fort constructed by the Maratha Empire. Built between 1757 and 1760 AD, it was designed by Sardar Krishnaji Madhavrao Panse, who was the chief of the Peshwa's artillery (Tofkhana).

The fort is named after Lord Malhari (Khandoba), the patron deity of many Maharashtrian families. It was strategically constructed to keep a close watch on the Dive Ghat pass and the route between Pune and Saswad. Although small in size, the fort features solid double-walled ramparts, ancient temples, and beautiful stepwells that make it a favorite for weekend trekkers. Exploring the ruins of the grand Panse Wada in the base village of Sonori provides a rare, close-up look at 18th-century Maratha residential architecture.

How to Reach

Road routes, nearest train connections, and bus pathways to reach the base village Sonori.

By Car / Road Trip

From Pune (30 km | 1 to 1.5 hours): Drive via Hadapsar -> Fursungi -> Dive Ghat. Immediately after descending Dive Ghat, take the left turn towards Sonori Village. The road is well-tarred and easily accessible for cars and bikes.
From Mumbai (175 km | 4 to 4.5 hours): Drive via Mumbai-Pune Expressway -> Pune Bypass (Katraj) -> Hadapsar -> Dive Ghat -> Sonori Village.

By Train

Nearest Station: Pune Junction Railway Station (32 km). Board local connecting trains or cabs from Pune Station to Hadapsar or Saswad Road, then hire local auto-rickshaws or state transport buses to Sonori village.

Pune Station: ~32 km

By Bus

MSRTC state transport buses run regularly from Swargate and Hadapsar Bus Stand in Pune to Saswad. From Saswad Bus Stand, you can hire local shared auto-rickshaws or connecting local transport buses to reach Sonori Village.

Ascent Choice: Once you reach Sonori village, you can start the trek directly. The trail is a gentle incline over rocky terrain. It takes about 30 to 45 minutes of easy climbing to reach the main fort entrance (Mahadarwaja).

Why Visit Malhargad Fort?

Malhargad Fort is a perfect blend of historic intrigue and natural beauty, making it one of the most rewarding short treks near Pune. As the final fort of the Marathas, it represents a unique era of Peshwa architecture, complete with defensive walls and fortified bastions that remain remarkably intact.

The trek itself is short and highly refreshing, especially during the monsoon when the surrounding valleys turn vibrant green. At the summit, you are greeted by twin temples, a beautifully carved pushkarini (stepwell), and a commanding 360-degree view of Saswad, Jejuri, and the Purandar Fort complex. Additionally, exploring the ruins of the grand Panse Wada in the base village of Sonori provides a rare, close-up look at 18th-century Maratha residential architecture.

Best Time to Visit

Malhargad Fort changes its colors with every season. Plan based on your preferred atmosphere.

Monsoon (Jun - Sep)

Highly recommended. The hill is covered in lush green carpets, and cool breezes flow across the plateau. The approach roads to Sonori can get muddy, but the trek trail is scenic and pleasant.

Winter (Oct - Feb)

Excellent for family outings. The climate is cool and comfortable, making it perfect for exploring the ruins and enjoying a picnic on the fort top under clear blue skies.

Summer (Mar - May)

Hot and dry. The afternoon sun can be harsh with no shade on the plateau. If visiting in summer, start early (around 6:00 AM) and carry plenty of hydrating drinks.

Difficulty & Suitability

Who can visit? Malhargad is highly accessible to all age groups due to the easy trail.

Easy / Beginner Friendly

The trek has a gentle slope with a mix of mud trails and stone steps. It requires very basic physical fitness, making it one of the easiest fort treks in Pune district.

Family & Kids Friendly

Since the ascent takes only 30-40 minutes, it is highly suitable for kids, elderly visitors who can walk short distances, and beginners looking to start trekking.

Visitor Type

Perfect for weekend picknickers, history enthusiasts, family groups, and amateur photographers. It is a peaceful getaway free from heavy tourist crowds.

Time Required

Plan your day to explore all landmarks without rushing.

Minimum Time Required

1.5 to 2 Hours: Enough to complete the trek from the base village to the top, explore the main temples, stepwell, and return.

Ideal Time Required

3 to 4 Hours: Highly recommended. This allows you to leisurely walk along the entire double-walled fortification, photograph the scenic views of Saswad valley, visit Panse Wada in Sonori village, and enjoy a packed snack at the top.

Total Trip Cost

Malhargad Fort is an extremely budget-friendly destination for weekend trips.

Basic Fees

Entry Fee: Absolutely Free. There is no official ticketing system to enter the fort.
Vehicle Parking: Nominal charge of ₹20 for two-wheelers and ₹50 for cars collected by local villagers at the base parking.

Guide & Transport

Local Guide: Usually not required as the trail is well-defined, but local village kids can show you around the fort and Panse Wada for ₹200 - ₹300.
Public Transport: Around ₹100 per person using local state buses and shared autos from Pune/Saswad.

Budget Estimates

Self-Drive / Trekker: ₹200 – ₹500 per person (covering fuel and snacks).
Family Outing: ₹800 – ₹1,500 total (including a meal at Saswad/Hadapsar).

Food Availability

Carry your own refreshments as food options on the fort are non-existent.

Eating Spots

There are no food stalls, tea shops, or water vendors on the fort top. In Sonori base village, you can find a couple of small shops selling packaged water and biscuits.

Local Specialties

For proper meals, head to nearby Dive Ghat highway dhabas or Saswad town, which is famous for traditional Maharashtrian dishes like Misal Pav, Bhakri-Pithla, and local sweets.

Water Supply

There is no potable drinking water source on the fort. Carrying at least 2 liters of water per person from Pune or Saswad is absolutely mandatory.

Facilities Available

Malhargad Fort provides minimal tourist services due to its quiet and heritage nature.

Washrooms & Parking

There are no public toilets on the fort top or at the immediate base trail. Basic parking is available in Sonori village. It is best to use restrooms at highway pumps or restaurants on the Saswad road.

ATMs & Cash

No ATMs are available in Sonori village. Carry sufficient cash for parking and local needs. The nearest ATMs are located in Hadapsar or Saswad.

Connectivity & Help

Good mobile network coverage (Jio, Airtel) is available throughout the trek and on the fort. For medical emergencies, Saswad (15 km) has primary health centers.

Safety Information

Key precautions to ensure visitor safety near walls and water bodies.

Danger Zones

The stone walls of the inner fort (Balekilla) are steep. Avoid climbing unsafe, crumbling structures. The stepwell (pushkarini) is deep and has slippery steps; avoid stepping into the water.

Prohibited Activities

Littering is strictly prohibited; carry all plastic waste back with you. Avoid drinking alcohol or smoking on the sacred fort grounds. Do not stray off the trail into thorny bushes.

Rainy Season Tips

The soil trail can become highly slippery and muddy during monsoons. Wear shoes with deep grooves/grip. Carry a windcheater or raincoat as the wind gets strong on the open plateau.

Things to Carry

Ensure you bring the right items for a comfortable day on the mountain fort.

Must-Carry Basics

At least 2 liters of drinking water, good grip trekking/running shoes, energy snacks (dates, energy bars), glucose powder (Glucon-D), and some cash.

Clothes & Protection

Cap, sunglasses, sunscreen (especially for summer/winter day treks), and rain jacket or windcheater (for monsoon treks).

Snacks & Power

Energy bars, dry fruits, glucose powder (ORS), camera gear, and a high-capacity power bank as mobile charging points are unavailable on the fort.

Best Photo Spots

Postcard locations to capture the grandeur and history of Malhargad Fort.

Balekilla Entrance

The main arched stone gateway of the inner fort provides a perfect historical frame.

Khandoba Temple

The twin stone temples situated side-by-side with the Sahyadri mountains in the background.

Pushkarini Stepwell

The beautifully carved, square stone stepwell that reflects the sky in its water.

Panse Wada (Sonori)

The grand Peshwa-era mansion ruins at the base village with fortified towers.

Insider Tips & Tricks

Practical suggestions from Maha Safar to enhance your visit.

Explore Sonori Wada

Don't just climb the fort. Spend 20 minutes exploring the Panse Wada in Sonori village at the base. It is a stunning historical structure with beautiful stone arches.

Sunrise / Early Morning

The fort is famous for its gorgeous sunrise views. Start early from Pune around 5:30 AM to catch the morning mist rising over Dive Ghat.

Combine with Jejuri

Since Jejuri temple town is only about 30 km from Saswad, you can easily combine your Malhargad trek with a visit to the famous Khandoba Temple of Jejuri.
