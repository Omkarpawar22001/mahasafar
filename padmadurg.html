About Fort

Padmadurg Fort, also known as Kasa Fort, is a unique sea fort built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in the Arabian Sea near Murud. Constructed to challenge the power of the Siddis of Janjira, the fort offers a rare island exploration experience with stunning sea views, historical structures, and coastal landscapes.

Built in 1676, it was designed in the shape of a lotus (Padma), serving as a crucial base to counter the unconquered Murud-Janjira fort. Today, it stands as a protected monument showing remnants of bastions and defense lines.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the marine defense island.

Massive Sea Walls

Strong basalt perimeter stone walls and bastions designed to withstand high ocean tides and cannon attacks.

Murud-Janjira Views

Stunning lookouts directly facing the famous impregnable marine fort of Murud-Janjira located nearby.

Island Surroundings

Dramatic coastal reefs and open ocean waves crashing against the rocky shores of Kasa island.

Ancient Ruins

Remnants of stone structures, chambers, and ammunition depots built inside the fortified grounds.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the island fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Bus & Boat via Murud

Take a direct bus from Mumbai to Murud. Alternatively, take a train to Roha Station and hire a taxi to Murud. From Murud beach or Rajpuri village, board local sailboats/motorboats to take you across to Padmadurg Fort (~20–30 minutes boat ride).

Cost: ₹400 – ₹900 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

A scenic ~160 km drive from Mumbai via the Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH 66) towards Murud. Head to Rajpuri village or Murud beach to board local boats. Note that boat operations depend heavily on weather conditions, sea tides, and local permissions.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth sea adventure.

Stay & Food

No accommodations or food options exist on the fort island. Abundant beachside resorts, homestays, and local coastal diners are available in Murud town.

Essentials

Carry drinking water, dry snacks, a cap, sunglasses, flat anti-slip footwear for wet rocks, a camera, and extra dry clothes.

Best Time

October to March is the best season. Boat transits are strictly closed during monsoon months (June to September) due to rough sea currents and heavy swells.
