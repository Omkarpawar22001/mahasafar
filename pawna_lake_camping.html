About the Campsite

Pawna Lake is one of Maharashtra's most popular organized camping destinations. Located near the scenic hill town of Lonavala, the beautiful lakeside setting offers gorgeous sunset views, cool evening breezes, and a peaceful weekend atmosphere.

Most campsites are situated directly beside the reservoir's edge, making it an ideal choice for couples, families, corporate groups, and friend circles. The surrounding landscape features a stunning historic backdrop, framed by the majestic Sahyadri forts of Tikona, Tung, Lohagad, and Visapur, making it a visual treat for nature lovers.

Package Details

Organised camps at Pawna Lake offer a hassle-free experience with comprehensive inclusions in a single booking.

Accommodation

Tent stay beside the lake, fitted with comfortable foam mats, soft pillows, and warm blankets for a cozy night under the stars.

Food & Snacks

Package includes evening high tea, marinated BBQ starters, a buffet dinner, and breakfast the following morning.

Entertainment

Access to lakeside activities, live music night setups, group bonfires, and a variety of indoor/outdoor sports.

Price & Booking

Campsites range from budget setups to premium luxury glamping structures, matching different comfort preferences.

Budget Camping

₹800 – ₹1,200 per person. Includes basic dome tents, common washrooms, standard buffet meals, bonfire, and music.

Premium setups

₹1,200 – ₹2,500 per person. Includes premium triangle or family tents, better lakeside spots, extra food options, and upgraded amenities.

Luxury Glamping

₹2,500+ per person. Includes large Swiss tents or lake-facing wooden cottages with attached private bathrooms, premium dining, and air conditioning.

Booking Tip: Advance online booking is highly recommended for weekend slots as popular campsites fill up quickly. Group discounts are frequently offered by organizers.

Accommodation Types

Various tent models are deployed to suit couples, large friend circles, and families.

Standard Tents

Dome & Triangle Tents: Waterproof multi-layer shelters. Perfect for couples and pairs seeking a classic camping style.

Large Groups

Family Tents: Spacious cabins designed to accommodate 4 to 6 people comfortably under a single rainfly structure.

Elevated Comfort

Swiss & Cottage units: Glamping setups and wooden cottages featuring comfortable regular beds and attached private toilets.

Meals Included

Packages feature a continuous cycle of fresh meals starting from afternoon check-in to checkout.

Check-in Snacks

Welcome tea or coffee served hot alongside local snacks like Pakodas or Misal Pav upon arriving at the campsite.

Evening BBQ

Freshly marinated items (paneer, potatoes, veggies, or chicken) roasted on charcoal grills, served warm in limited portions.

Dinner & Breakfast

Unlimited buffet dinner featuring local Maharashtrian dishes (Veg/Non-Veg). Jain food choices are available at most major camps upon early request.

Activities Included

Campsites offer a wide array of recreational activities to keep visitors engaged throughout their stay.

Water Sports

Kayaking on the lake edges, paddle boating, and calm rowboat rides. Life jackets are mandatory and provided by organizers.

Field Games

Access to sports gear for volleyball, cricket, badminton, football, and archery targets set up in the open fields.

Night Vibe

Enjoying starry clear skies, stargazing sessions, live guitar performance circles, and a warm central bonfire.

Facilities Available

Organized campsites are fully fenced and equipped with basic amenities to ensure comfort.

Washrooms

Sanitized toilet blocks (both Western and Indian formats) located closely, separated for gents and ladies.

Power & Water

Dedicated charging docks for electronic gear. Clean drinking water filters are installed in the dining pavilion.

Dining & Parking

Secure parking lots directly at the campground entrance. Covered common dining pavilions are provided for eating meals.

Safety & Security

Unlike wild self-camping, these sites provide a secure environment suitable for all traveler groups.

Dedicated Staff

Sites are monitored by onsite security personnel and hosts. Emergency contact coordinates are displayed clearly.

Medical Support

Basic first-aid boxes, antiseptic lotions, and bandages are always available with the camp organizer desk.

Well-Lit Zones

Fenced premises with floodlighting. Pathway lights ensure travelers can safely navigate the campground during the night.

Group Suitability

Organized campsites maintain a friendly, inclusive atmosphere tailored to various traveler types.

Couples & Families

Dedicated zones to ensure privacy and safety. Safe playing fields for children and comfortable dining spaces for families.

Corporates

Ideal spaces for team-building events, outdoor workshops, games, and large group dining layouts.

Friends & Solo

Great social environment with campfire music, stargazing circles, and opportunity to mingle with fellow campers.

How to Reach

Details to reach Pawna Lake from major cities in Maharashtra.

From Pune

Drive via Kamshet (Old Mumbai-Pune highway) and take the Kamshet-Pawna Lake Road. The route is scenic and takes around 1.5 hours.

Pune to Pawna: ~55 km

From Mumbai

Drive along the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, exit at Lonavala, and proceed via Dudhiware Khind road or Kamshet route. The drive takes around 2.5 to 3 hours.

Mumbai to Pawna: ~120 km

From Lonavala Station

If traveling by train, get down at Lonavala station. Local private jeeps and cabs can be hired directly to the Pawna lake camps.

Lonavala to Pawna: ~25 km

Things to Carry

A handy checklist of items to bring for a comfortable lakeside camping overnight stay.

ID & Medicine

Valid government photo identity card (mandatory for camp check-in), personal prescription medications, and mosquito repellent cream.

Clothing & Gear

A light jacket or sweater (temperatures drop beside the water at night), extra pair of clothes, sports shoes, and a torch.

Electronics

A fully charged power bank (charging slots at camps can be limited), camera gear, and dry bags to keep devices safe near water.

Best Time to Visit

Pawna Lake has distinct seasonal vibes, each offering a unique outdoor experience.

Winter (Oct - Feb)

Peak Season: Highly recommended. Fantastic weather, cool breezes, and clear night skies ideal for stargazing and campfires.

Monsoon (Jun - Sep)

Lush Vibe: The surrounding hills and forts turn lush green. Ideal for travelers who enjoy heavy rains and misty landscapes.

Weekdays

Quiet Option: If you prefer peace and solitude over party crowds and loud music, weekday camping is highly recommended.

Weekend Vibe

Prepare yourself for the different crowd dynamics between weekends and weekdays.

Lively Atmosphere

Saturdays feature high crowd levels, loud music setups, social events, and a festival-like vibe. Excellent for groups looking to party and socialize.

Advance Bookings

Due to high popularity, booking campsites weeks in advance is highly recommended for Saturday stays. Unplanned drive-ins may find popular camps fully booked.

Nearby Attractions

Historic forts and caves located within a short driving distance from the lake.

Tikona & Tung Forts

Tikona (Vitandgad) is famous for its triangular shape and stone steps, while Tung Fort stands as a conical peak directly overlooking Pawna reservoir.

Lohagad & Visapur

Lohagad Fort is an easily accessible fortress with ancient gates, connected to the massive plateau ruins of Visapur Fort.

Bhaja Caves

Located near Malavli, this site features 22 historic rock-cut Buddhist caves dating back to the 2nd century BC.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Terms vary between organizers, but standard weekend rules apply across the lake camps.

Payments

Most camp organizers collect advance booking deposits. Weekend bookings (especially Saturday nights) are usually non-refundable.

Date Changes

Date changes might be accommodated by organizers if informed 3 to 5 days in advance, subject to availability. Always read the organizer policy prior to booking.
