About Fort

Prabalgad Fort is a historic hill fort located near Panvel in Maharashtra, close to the famous Kalavantin Durg. It was once used as a watchtower fort due to its strategic location overlooking the Konkan region.

The trek passes through dense forests and offers panoramic Sahyadri views, making it a great destination for nature lovers and beginner trekkers. Unlike the adjacent Kalavantin Durg which features narrow rock steps, Prabalgad has a massive, flat forest plateau top with ruins of ancient stone chambers.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the strategic watchtower fort.

Prabalgad Plateau

A massive, flat, forested plateau top holding ruins of stone buildings and a fresh water pond.

Kalavantin Durg Views

Incredible vantage points offering a direct, bird's-eye view of the spiral stairs and peak of Kalavantin Durg.

Forest Trails

Lush green pathways rich in local biodiversity, passing through dense deciduous forest zones.

Sahyadri Panorama

Panoramic lookouts overlooking the surrounding fields, Panvel city, and distant mountain ridges.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the base village from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Auto (Thakurwadi Village)

Take a Mumbai local train to Panvel Station. From Panvel (East), hire a local auto-rickshaw or take shared vans directly to Thakurwadi village base area (~15 km).

Cost: ₹100 – ₹200 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

A quick ~50 km drive from Mumbai via the Sion-Panvel Expressway, exiting at Panvel towards Thakurwadi village. Secure vehicle parking spaces are available near the base of the trek.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

No proper stays exist on the fort plateau. Simple homestays and camping sites are available at Prabalmachi (the midway plateau). Villagers run small food stalls serving local meals.

Essentials

Carry 3 liters water, trekking shoes with good grip, a cap, sunscreen, a raincoat (monsoon), a flashlight/torch, and a basic personal first aid kit.

Best Time

October to February is the most pleasant season for trekking. Monsoon season (June to September) is also extremely scenic but the climbing trails can get muddy and slippery.

Location & Coordinates
