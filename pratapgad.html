About Fort

Pratapgad Fort is a historically significant fort located near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra. It is famous for the Battle of Pratapgad between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Afzal Khan.

Surrounded by dense forests and Sahyadri mountain views, the fort is easily accessible and ideal for history lovers, beginners, and families. Built by Shivaji Maharaj in 1656, it was strategic for controlling the local region.

Major Attractions

Explore the historical battle sites and religious monuments at Pratapgad.

Bhavani Mata Temple

An ancient temple built by Shivaji Maharaj, housing a beautiful idol of Goddess Bhavani.

Upper & Lower Fort

The dual fortification structures containing chambers, double-layered walls, and viewpoints.

Afzal Khan Tomb

The mausoleum of Afzal Khan located at the foot of the fort, marking a historic encounter.

Viewpoints & Statue

Vantage points offering views of the Koyna Valley and a bronze statue of Shivaji Maharaj.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Road via Satara

Take a train from Mumbai to Satara Railway Station. From Satara, take local state transport (ST) buses or hire a taxi to reach Mahabaleshwar, then travel to Pratapgad Fort (~22 km from Mahabaleshwar).

Cost: ₹300 – ₹800 Approx

Option 2: Direct Bus to Mahabaleshwar

Board a direct state transport (MSRTC) bus or a private luxury sleeper bus from Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar. From Mahabaleshwar, local tourist cabs are easily available to the fort.

Cost: ₹500 – ₹1200 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~220 km road trip via the Pune-Kolhapur Highway (NH 48), Wai, and Pasarni Ghat. A fully motorable road is available almost all the way to the top of the fort, where parking facilities are available.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Excellent luxury hotels, homestays, and budget lodges are available in the nearby hill station of Mahabaleshwar. Simple local food stalls are available near the fort entrance.

Essentials

Wear comfortable walking shoes to climb the fort steps. Carry water bottles, a cap, sunscreen, a light jacket, and a camera to capture the stunning views.

Best Time

October to February is ideal for pleasant weather. The monsoon season (June to September) is spectacular, showing lush green valley views and misty cloud covers.

Location & Coordinates

Pratapgad Fort, Near Mahabaleshwar, Satara District, Maharashtra, India
