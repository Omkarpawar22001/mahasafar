About Fort

Raigad Fort is one of the most historically significant forts in Maharashtra and served as the capital of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. It is the place where Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as the king of the Maratha Empire.

Located at an altitude of around 820 meters, the fort offers a mix of history, architecture, and scenic beauty. Unlike many other forts, Raigad has both trekking and ropeway options, making it accessible for all kinds of travelers.

Major Attractions

Explore the architectural wonders of the capital.

Raj Bhavan

The majestic ruins of the King's Palace, where historical decisions were made.

Shivaji Samadhi

The final resting place of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, a place of immense respect.

Takmak Tok

A thrilling cliff viewpoint offering panoramic views of the Sahyadri range.

Jagdishwar Temple

A beautiful temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, reflecting ancient Maratha art.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: The Rail Route

Take a train from Mumbai (CSMT/Dadar) to Mangaon (~3 hrs). From Mangaon, shared jeeps or autos are easily available to Pachad village (~45 min).

Cost: ₹250 – ₹700 Approx

Option 2: The Bus Route

Take a ST or Private Bus from Mumbai to Mahad (5–6 hrs). From Mahad, local jeeps connect directly to the Pachad base village.

Cost: ₹500 – ₹1000 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic 170 km drive (4–5 hrs) via the Mumbai–Goa highway. Ample parking is available near the Pachad base village.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Hotels and homestays are available at Pachad and Mahad. Basic food is available at both top and base. MTDC resort is available for a comfortable stay.

Essentials

Carry 2–3L water, trekking shoes, cap, and sunscreen. During monsoon, a raincoat is mandatory. Carry a power bank.

Best Time

October to February is ideal. Monsoon (June-Sept) for lush greenery. Avoid summer afternoons.
