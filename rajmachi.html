About Fort

Rajmachi Fort is one of the most popular trekking and camping destinations near Mumbai and Pune. It consists of two forts, Shrivardhan and Manaranjan, surrounded by dense forests and valleys.

Rajmachi is famous for its fireflies festival, scenic trekking routes, and peaceful village atmosphere at Udhewadi. It offers hikers a chance to camp under star-studded skies and walk ancient trade paths.

Twin Forts

Shrivardhan & Manaranjan

Base Village

Udhewadi

Major Attractions

Explore the historical structures and unique natural events at Rajmachi.

Shrivardhan Fort

The taller twin fort offering panoramic views of the entire valley and historic ruins.

Manaranjan Fort

The secondary fort containing old chambers, water reservoirs, and stone ramparts.

Fireflies Festival

A magical pre-monsoon event (May-June) where millions of fireflies illuminate the forest.

Monsoon Waterfalls

Spectacular waterfalls cascading down the surrounding valleys and cliffs.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Train & Trek via Lonavala

Take a train from Mumbai to Lonavala Station. From Lonavala, start the 14-16 km flat trail to Rajmachi (Udhewadi village). Local shared jeeps are also available from Lonavala to Udhewadi.

Cost: ₹150 – ₹400 Approx

Option 2: Karjat & Kondane Cave Route

Board a local train to Karjat Station, then take local transport to Kondivade village. Trek up via the Kondane Caves trail (3-4 hrs of uphill trek to the fort).

Cost: ₹200 – ₹450 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

Drive to Lonavala, then proceed along the rough motorable road to Udhewadi base village. A 4x4 or high-clearance SUV is recommended for this stretch.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Homestays and camping sites are widely available in Udhewadi village. Local villagers serve simple, delicious Maharashtrian meals.

Essentials

Carry a torch, comfortable trekking shoes, a raincoat (in monsoon), a sleeping bag (if camping), and sufficient water bottles.

Best Time

June to February is ideal. May to early June is the peak season for the Fireflies Festival. Monsoon offers spectacular green views and waterfalls.
