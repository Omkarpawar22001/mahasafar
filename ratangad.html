About Fort

Ratangad Fort is a beautiful hill fort located in the Sahyadri ranges near Bhandardara. It is famous for its natural rock cave called Nedhe (Eye of the Needle) and its excellent camping experience.

Surrounded by forests, valleys, and scenic landscapes, Ratangad offers breathtaking sunrise, sunset, and stargazing opportunities. Over 400 years old, it was once captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and holds immense heritage value.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of Ratangad Fort's ancient landscape.

Nedhe (Needle Eye)

A natural wind-carved circular cavity in the rock face that you can climb inside and view both sides of the valley.

Cave Stay on Fort

Natural caves that are clean, dry, and spacious enough to host overnight stays and camping experiences.

Walls & Gates

Historic stone gates (Ganesh, Hanuman, Konkan, and Tryambak) guarding the fortified boundaries.

AMK & Lake Views

Incredible sightlines overlooking Bhandardara Lake, Kalsubai Peak, and the rugged Alang-Madan-Kulang forts.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Jeep (Bari Village)

Take a train from Mumbai to Kasara station (~2 hours). From Kasara, hire a shared local taxi/jeep to Bari village, which serves as the primary base village for the trek.

Cost: ₹200 – ₹450 Approx

Option 2: Transit via Igatpuri / Ghoti

Take a train or bus from Mumbai to Igatpuri or Ghoti town. From there, local state transport (ST) buses and private jeeps run frequently towards Bhandardara and Bari village.

Cost: ₹250 – ₹600 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~165 km drive from Mumbai along the Mumbai-Nasik Highway (NH 3), exiting at Ghoti towards Bhandardara Lake and Bari village. Secure vehicle parking is available near the base of the trek.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Overnight cave stays are highly popular. You can also camp in tents. Local villagers provide wood fire cooked dinner, breakfast, and tea on order near the caves.

Essentials

Carry 3–4L water, good grip trekking shoes, a warm sleeping bag/blanket, a torch, a raincoat/jacket, and a basic personal first aid kit.

Best Time

October to February is ideal for cooler temperatures and clear stargazing. Monsoon (June-Sept) offers breathtaking mist, waterfalls, and vibrant green valleys.

Location & Coordinates
