About Fort

Sagargad Fort is a beautiful hill fort located near Alibaug in Maharashtra. It is known for its scenic trekking route through forests, waterfalls, and villages. The fort offers stunning views of the Arabian Sea and surrounding landscapes, making it a peaceful destination for nature lovers and beginner trekkers.

Unlike other high-altitude peaks, Sagargad offers a distinct coastal charm where you can witness the beautiful Alibaug coastline. The trail winds through dense woods, passing by local temples and historic fortifications that stand silent testimony to the region's rich history.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of this coastal hill fort trek.

Waterfall Near Base

A seasonal gushing waterfall located near Khandale village, offering a great cooling point during monsoon.

Fort Ruins & Walls

Explore ancient stone fortification ruins, walls, and old water tanks remaining on the fort.

Alibaug Coastline

Vantage points offering a spectacular panoramic overview of the Arabian Sea and Alibaug coast.

Siddheshwar Temple

A quiet, old temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located midway along the trekking trail.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the base village from Mumbai.

Option 1: Ferry & Local Transport

Take a ferry from Gateway of India to Mandwa Jetty. Board the operators' connecting bus to Alibaug. From Alibaug, hire an auto-rickshaw or take a local ST bus to Khandale base village (~7 km).

Cost: ₹150 – ₹300 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

Drive ~100 km from Mumbai via the Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH66) and Pen-Alibaug Road, turning towards Khandale base village. Secure vehicle parking spaces are available.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

No stay options exist on the fort itself. Excellent hotels, beach resorts, and homestays are available in Alibaug. Local food stalls are present in Khandale village.

Essentials

Carry 3 liters of water, sturdy trekking shoes, a cap, sunscreen, a raincoat (monsoon), and a basic first aid kit.

Best Time

June to February. Monsoon (June-Sept) is ideal for waterfalls and greenery, while winter (Oct-Feb) is great for cool coastal views.

Location & Coordinates
