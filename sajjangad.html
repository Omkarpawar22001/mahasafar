About Fort

Sajjangad Fort is a sacred and historic fort located near Satara in Maharashtra. It is famous as the final resting place of Sant Ramdas, the spiritual guru of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

More of a spiritual destination than a trekking challenge, it offers peaceful surroundings, religious importance, and scenic Sahyadri views. The name 'Sajjangad' translates to the 'Fort of Good People'.

Major Attractions

Explore the holy shrines and peaceful complex inside Sajjangad Fort.

Sant Ramdas Samadhi

The sacred final resting place and shrine of the revered saint Sant Ramdas.

Temple Complex

Ancient stone temples hosting daily prayers and serving free prasad meals.

Fort Walls & Gates

Robust stone defensive gates and fortification ramparts protecting the holy grounds.

Valley Viewpoints

High viewpoints offering spectacular, peaceful views of the surrounding Sahyadri hills.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Transit from Satara

Take a train or bus from Mumbai to Satara city. From Satara Railway Station or bus stand, local buses and auto-rickshaws are frequently available to reach the Sajjangad base village (~18 km).

Cost: ₹150 – ₹350 Approx

Option 2: Direct Bus Routes

Government ST buses run directly from Satara city stand to the foothill of Sajjangad. Shared autos are also a popular choice.

Cost: ₹50 – ₹100 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~260 km road trip from Mumbai via the Pune-Satara Highway (NH 48). A fully motorable road is available almost all the way to the top, with a parking lot located near the entrance.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Free/donation-based basic accommodation is available at the temple complex. Free afternoon prasad meals are served daily to all devotees and visitors. Hotels are available in Satara city.

Essentials

Wear comfortable walking shoes to climb the 200+ stone steps leading to the fort. Dress in respectful clothing suitable for a place of worship. Carry water and a camera.

Best Time

October to February is ideal for pleasant weather. Monsoons are also beautiful with misty hill ranges and rolling clouds surrounding the holy fort.
