About Fort

Tung Fort is a small but scenic hill fort located near Pawna Lake close to Lonavala. It is known for its narrow ridge and cone-shaped structure, offering stunning panoramic views.

The fort is surrounded by water on three sides, giving it a unique island-like feel and making it a perfect one-day trek destination. The climb is short but steep, leading to a rewarding conical viewpoint.

Major Attractions

Explore the unique features and viewpoints of Tung Fort.

Pawna Lake Views

Breathtaking sights of the vast Pawna dam reservoir surrounding the fort on three sides.

Narrow Ridge Walk

A thrilling hike along the narrow grassy ridge leading up to the main fort structures.

Fort Top Viewpoint

The sharp conical summit offering 360-degree views of Tikona, Lohagad, and Visapur.

Sahyadri Hills

Sweeping vistas of the rolling mountain ranges and lush green valleys of Lonavala.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Local Transit

Take a train from Mumbai to Lonavala Station. From Lonavala, hire a local auto-rickshaw or private jeep to travel to the Tung base village.

Cost: ₹300 – ₹700 Approx

Option 2: Direct Drive

A scenic ~110 km drive from Mumbai (2.5–3 hrs) via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and Pawna Dam Road. Parking facilities are available near the base village.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

No stay facilities are available on the fort. Lakeside camping sites and premium resorts are widely available around Pawna Lake. Basic snacks can be bought at the base village.

Essentials

Carry 2L water, energy snacks, sturdy trekking shoes with good grip, a cap, sunscreen, and a raincoat (in monsoon).

Best Time

June to February is ideal. The monsoon season (June-Sept) features lush green landscapes, while the winter months (Oct-Feb) offer pleasant cooler weather.

Location & Coordinates
