About Fort

Vasota Fort is a unique jungle trek located inside the Koyna Wildlife Sanctuary in Maharashtra. It is famous for its combination of a boat ride across Shivsagar Lake and a scenic forest trek.

Surrounded by dense forests, wildlife, and stunning Sahyadri views, it is an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers. The trek requires forest permissions, making it a pure wilderness experience.

Location

Koyna Sanctuary

Base Village

Bamnoli Village

Major Attractions

Explore the dense jungle paths and panoramic viewpoints of Vasota Fort.

Shivsagar Lake Boat Ride

A scenic launch boat ride across Koyna backwaters to reach the forest checkpoint.

Dense Forest Trails

Pristine walking trails inside the sanctuary under tall forest tree canopies.

Vasota Fort Ruins

Historic ruins of the mountain fort, dating back to Shilahara dynasty builders.

Babu Kada Viewpoint

A thrilling vertical cliff drop offering panoramic views of Koyna backwaters.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Road via Satara

Take a train or bus from Mumbai to Satara city. From Satara, board local state transport (ST) buses or hire a private taxi to reach Bamnoli village (~1.5 hrs, 38 km).

Cost: ₹300 – ₹600 Approx

Option 2: Shivsagar Lake Boat Ride

From Bamnoli village, obtain necessary forest permissions and hire a shared motorboat to travel across Shivsagar Lake (~1-1.5 hrs boat ride) to reach the trek starting point.

Cost: ₹400 – ₹800 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~280 km drive from Mumbai via the Pune-Satara Highway (NH 48) to Bamnoli. Secure vehicle parking facilities are available at Bamnoli base village.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Overnight stay is strictly prohibited inside the wildlife sanctuary. Comfortable hotels, homestays, and lakeside camps are available at Bamnoli village and Satara city. Carry your own food pack for the trek.

Essentials

Carry 2-3L water, lunch pack/snacks, strong trekking shoes, insect repellent, a cap, sunscreen, a raincoat, a torch, and a basic first aid kit.

Best Time

October to February is ideal. The sanctuary is closed to visitors during the monsoon season (usually from mid-June to September) for safety.

Location & Coordinates
