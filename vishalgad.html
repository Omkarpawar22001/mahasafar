About Fort

Vishalgad Fort is a historic hill fort located in Kolhapur district of Maharashtra. It played an important role in the history of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and is famous for its connection to the escape from Panhala Fort.

Surrounded by lush greenery and scenic Sahyadri views, it is easily accessible and suitable for all travelers. The fort acts as an incredible window into the heritage of the Maratha Empire.

Major Attractions

Explore the highlights of the historical fort ruins.

Baji Prabhu Memorial

A majestic tribute dedicated to the legendary Maratha commander Baji Prabhu Deshpande.

Amruteshwar Temple

An ancient temple built in Hemadpanti architectural style, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Fort Walls & Gates

Historic stone fortifications and gates that protected the fort through the centuries.

Viewpoints & Ruins

Vantage points offering panoramic views of the Sahyadri mountains and historic ruins.

Travel Guide

Comprehensive routes to reach the fort from Mumbai.

Option 1: Rail & Road Route

Take a train from Mumbai to Kolhapur Railway Station (8–10 hrs). From Kolhapur, take a state transport (ST) bus or hire a private taxi to reach the Vishalgad fort (~2–3 hrs, 76 km).

Cost: ₹300 – ₹800 Approx

Option 2: Bus Routes

Board a direct government MSRTC bus or a private luxury sleeper bus from Mumbai to Kolhapur city (9–12 hrs), then take local transport to the fort.

Cost: ₹600 – ₹1200 Approx

Option 3: Direct Drive

A scenic ~430 km road trip via the Pune-Kolhapur Highway (NH 48) and Amba Ghat. The road goes almost all the way to the top with parking facilities near the main gate.

Essential Information

Everything you need to know for a smooth adventure.

Stay & Food

Food stalls offering local spicy Kolhapuri meals and snacks are available near the fort. Accommodations on the fort are basic; comfortable hotels and lodges are available in Kolhapur city.

Essentials

Carry 2–3L water, comfortable shoes, a cap, sunscreen, and a camera. Carrying rain gear during the monsoons (June-Sept) is highly recommended.

Best Time

October to February is ideal for exploration. The monsoon season (June to September) offers lush greenery, rolling clouds, and spectacular valley views.

Location & Coordinates

Vishalgad Fort, Bhudargad Taluka, Kolhapur District, Maharashtra, India
